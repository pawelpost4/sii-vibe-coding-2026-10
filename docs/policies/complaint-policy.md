# Complaint Policy (sample)

> **Sample document for the Hardware Service Decision Copilot MVP.** It describes the complaint rules of a fictional electronics retailer. It is inspired by the Polish Consumer Rights Act (seller's liability for non-conformity of goods with the contract) but is not legal advice and has not been reviewed by a lawyer. Replace it with the company's real policy before production use.

Version: 1.0 — 2026-10-18

## 1. Scope

1.1. This policy covers complaints about electronics equipment sold to consumers that is defective or does not conform to the contract (for example, it does not work, works incorrectly or lacks declared features).

1.2. A customer who simply does not want the equipment, and reports no defect, files a return under the Return Policy.

1.3. A manufacturer's warranty (gwarancja) is separate and voluntary. The customer may use it instead of this policy; this policy does not limit it.

## 2. Liability period

2.1. The company is liable for non-conformity that **becomes apparent within 2 years from the date of purchase**.

2.2. A defect that becomes apparent within those 2 years is **presumed to have existed at the date of purchase**, unless the company proves otherwise or the presumption cannot be reconciled with the nature of the equipment or the defect (for example, damage clearly caused by an external impact).

2.3. A complaint about a defect that first appeared more than 2 years after purchase is **rejected** under this policy. The customer is informed that a manufacturer's warranty may still apply.

## 3. Covered defects (examples)

3.1. The equipment does not turn on, restarts or freezes without external cause.

3.2. Failure of internal components: battery that swells or does not hold charge far below specification, faulty charging port with no signs of mechanical damage, dead pixels or lines on a screen with no impact marks, failing speakers, microphones, buttons or hinges under normal use.

3.3. Manufacturing defects visible on the housing: gaps, misaligned parts, delamination of coatings without signs of impact.

3.4. Missing features or parameters declared in the product description.

## 4. Excluded damage

The company is not liable for damage caused after purchase by the customer or third parties. A complaint is **rejected** when the photo or description clearly shows:

4.1. **Mechanical damage** — cracked or shattered screens or housings with an impact point, dents, bent frames, broken-off parts, typical drop damage on corners.

4.2. **Liquid damage** — corrosion, water stains, liquid under the screen.

4.3. **Unauthorized repair or modification** — missing or broken warranty seals, non-original parts, opened housing, missing screws.

4.4. **Improper use** — use against the manual, wrong charger or voltage, overheating from blocked ventilation, burn marks from external sources.

4.5. **Normal wear and tear** — cosmetic wear of surfaces, keys or cables consistent with the age and normal use of the equipment, with no loss of function.

## 5. Decisions

| Decision | When |
|---|---|
| **Accepted** | Within 2 years, the described defect is consistent with the photo, and no exclusion under section 4 is visible. |
| **Rejected** | After 2 years, or the photo clearly shows an excluded cause under section 4. |
| **Service inspection required** | The defect is internal or functional and cannot be confirmed or ruled out from a photo (for example, "does not charge" with no visible damage), or the cause is uncertain. |
| **Escalated to a specialist** | The description contradicts the photo, information is inconsistent, or the case is not covered by this policy. |

## 6. Remedies and next steps

6.1. For an accepted complaint the customer may request **repair or replacement**. The company may choose the other remedy if the requested one is impossible or disproportionately costly.

6.2. A **price reduction or withdrawal from the contract** (full refund) is available when repair or replacement is impossible, was refused, failed, or the defect is serious. Withdrawal is not available for a minor non-conformity.

6.3. The company responds to every complaint within **14 calendar days** from receiving it. No response within that period means the complaint is accepted.

6.4. Equipment sent for service inspection is collected from the customer by the employee with a written intake record (condition, accessories, reported defect).

6.5. For a rejected complaint the customer is told the reason with a reference to the relevant section of this policy and, where relevant, about the option of a paid repair or the manufacturer's warranty.
