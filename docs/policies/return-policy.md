# Return Policy (sample)

> **Sample document for the Hardware Service Decision Copilot MVP.** It describes the return rules of a fictional electronics retailer. It is inspired by Polish consumer law but is not legal advice and has not been reviewed by a lawyer. Replace it with the company's real policy before production use.

Version: 1.0 — 2026-10-18

## 1. Scope

1.1. This policy covers returns of electronics equipment bought by consumers from the company, in-store or online, when the customer withdraws from the purchase **without claiming a defect**.

1.2. A customer who reports a defect or damage files a complaint, not a return. Complaints follow the Complaint Policy.

## 2. Return period

2.1. The customer may return equipment within **14 calendar days** from the date of purchase. For online purchases this is the statutory right of withdrawal from a distance contract; for in-store purchases the company grants the same period voluntarily.

2.2. A return submitted on day 15 or later after the date of purchase is **rejected**.

2.3. The day of purchase is day 0. Example: equipment bought on 1 October may be returned until 15 October inclusive.

## 3. Condition of returned equipment

The company resells returned equipment as new. A return is accepted only when the equipment is in resellable condition:

3.1. **No physical damage** — no scratches, dents, cracks, chips, bent parts, pressure marks or broken elements.

3.2. **No signs of use beyond checking the product** — the customer may unpack and switch on the equipment to check its features, as in a store. Not acceptable: wear on keys, buttons or ports, fingerprints or dirt that do not wipe off, worn surfaces, stickers or residue, removed or replaced parts.

3.3. **Screens and displays** — no scratches, dead pixels caused by pressure, or burn-in.

3.4. **Completeness** — the equipment is returned with all accessories, cables, manuals and gifts received with it. Missing items are listed in the decision and must be supplied before the refund.

3.5. **Original packaging** is preferred but not required. A damaged or missing box does not by itself cause rejection.

## 4. Items excluded from returns

4.1. Equipment delivered in sealed packaging that cannot be returned for health or hygiene reasons once the seal is broken — **in-ear headphones and earbuds** whose hygienic seal has been opened.

4.2. Equipment made or modified to the customer's specification (for example, engraved devices).

4.3. Sealed software or digital content whose seal has been opened.

## 5. Decisions

| Decision | When |
|---|---|
| **Accepted** | Within 14 days, no damage, no signs of use beyond checking, not excluded under section 4. |
| **Rejected** | After 14 days, visible damage, clear signs of use, or excluded under section 4. |
| **Service inspection required** | Condition cannot be confirmed from the photo (for example, the relevant side or the screen is not visible), or possible hidden damage needs a hands-on check. |
| **Escalated to a specialist** | Information is contradictory or the case is not covered by this policy. |

## 6. Next steps after an accepted return

6.1. The employee checks completeness against the original order and collects the equipment.

6.2. The refund is issued within **14 calendar days** from receiving the equipment, using the original payment method.

6.3. If a damaged item is accepted by mistake and the damage is found on intake, the case is re-evaluated by a specialist.

## 7. Next steps after a rejected return

7.1. The customer is told the reason, with a reference to the relevant section of this policy.

7.2. If the customer reports a defect, they are informed that they may file a complaint under the Complaint Policy.
